Grapsy · Your data

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This notice explains how Grapsy handles information when you use the Python teacher, coding tools, profile, projects, and community features. Grapsy is developed by Prakash Shah in Nepal.

Information Grapsy handles

  • Account information: name, username, email address, login provider, password hash for password accounts, your accepted Terms version and timestamp, and profile details you choose to add, such as phone number, birth date, location, or profile picture.
  • Learning information: lesson activity, prompts, hints, submitted code, feedback, progress, and saved practice history.
  • Community information: public projects, follow relationships, direct messages, feedback, blocks, and safety reports.
  • Basic technical information: information needed to keep your session and the website working, including standard requests handled by the hosting provider.

How information is used

Grapsy uses this information to provide accounts, lessons, progress reports, code feedback, saved projects, messaging, support, and abuse prevention. Grapsy does not sell personal information.

AI chat and code

The Grapsy Python teacher currently uses Botpress Webchat. Messages, learning prompts, and code you send to the teacher may be processed by Botpress to provide the conversation. Review the Botpress Privacy Statement. Do not include passwords, API keys, private records, or other secrets in chat or code you submit for AI review.

The Run Code feature loads the Pyodide Python runtime from jsDelivr and executes code in your browser. That code is not sent to jsDelivr by the runner. If you submit code to the teacher for feedback, the code may be sent to the AI chat provider.

Public and private content

Projects marked Public are visible to other Grapsy users. Private projects are shown only in your account. Your username, display name, and profile picture may be shown with public projects and community interactions. Direct messages are for the participants in that conversation, subject to safety reports and applicable service operation.

Service providers

Grapsy relies on service providers for AI chat, hosting, email delivery, and sign-in when you choose Google or Facebook. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Information may be processed in countries where those providers operate.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while needed to provide the service. You can edit profile details, make projects private, and delete your account from Profile. Account deletion removes the account’s profile, projects, learning history, follows, blocks, and direct messages. Safety reports may be retained for review; account identifiers are detached from those reports when an account is deleted, while a reported username and relevant message excerpt may remain in the report record.

Security and your choices

Grapsy uses account controls and technical measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. Use a unique password, keep credentials out of code, and use the Safety Center to block or report an account.

For reference, the Nepal Law Commission publishes the Privacy Act, 2075. This notice does not certify legal compliance.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email grapsy.app@gmail.com or use the Grapsy feedback page.

This notice should be updated if Grapsy changes its data practices or providers. It describes the current product behavior and is not a statement that every legal requirement has been independently certified.
