Block an account

  • Blocking removes the follow connection in both directions.
  • Neither account can message or follow the other while the block is active.
  • You will no longer see each other’s public community projects in Grapsy.
  • Unblocking does not restore follows; either person can follow again later.

Report a concern

Use the Report button on a user or message to tell Grapsy’s operator about harassment, spam, inappropriate content, or a safety concern. Reports are limited to ten per account in a 24-hour period. A moderator can review submitted reports and update their status.

For urgent danger, contact local emergency services. Grapsy is not an emergency response service.

Questions or privacy requests: grapsy.app@gmail.com.
