A teacher that changes its mind about what you need

Every answer updates what Grapsy knows about you. The loop repeats until each topic sticks.

  1. Take the diagnostic

    Answer a short set of questions. You get a result for each topic, not one grade for everything.

  2. Get your plan

    Grapsy builds a learning path from your goal, your weak topics, and the time you have each week.

  3. Study a short lesson

    Each lesson has an explanation, a worked example, and hints that start small.

  4. Practice and get feedback

    When an answer is wrong, you learn why, with a new example for that exact mistake.

  5. See your mastery change

    Topic scores move as you improve, so you can see what you've actually learned.

  6. Review before you forget

    Topics you missed come back later, until you get them right on your own.
